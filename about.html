Our story.

We love cafe hopping, but finding places that actually hit the spot shouldn’t feel like searching for a missing semicolon in a thousand lines of Java.

Maps is great at finding what’s famous, but there was never a dedicated space to discover the hidden, low-key, niche places that Surat actually has to offer. So we built WHERE? as a passion project—a raw, personal guide made by gossip queens.
